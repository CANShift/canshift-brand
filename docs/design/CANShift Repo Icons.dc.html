REPOSITORY ICONS

One mark per repo, built on the CS grid.

Same construction as the monogram — 116 × 100 frame, 11° shear, one red element per mark. Three stroke weights only: 13 u for signal, 7 u for rules and frames, 18 u for handles — 15 and 22 u at small sizes. Five repos under the CANShift org.

THE FAMILY RULE
Exactly one red element per mark, and it always names what the repo does.
Never two accents, never a red outline. Below 24 px only the primary strokes survive. On dark grounds the accent becomes #FF4747, like the dash.

The CS monogram stays the umbrella mark — org avatar, splash screen, the icon on the dash itself. A repo mark never replaces it; it identifies one piece inside the system.

Using them

Each mark is a single SVG with two path groups — ink and accent. Swap the ink for currentColor to let it follow the surrounding text, and switch the accent to #FF4747 on dark grounds.

In the docs sidebar they sit at 16 px next to their section, on the org page they are the repo avatars at 460 px, and the mobile mark is the app icon at 1024. Only the small sizes need the second drawing: the shear closes up and the secondary rules are dropped.
